Common Roofing Problems Homeowners Face

The climate in South Carolina can create several roofing challenges for homeowners. Heavy rain, strong winds, humidity, and intense heat can gradually weaken roofing materials over time. One of the most common problems is roof leaks caused by damaged shingles or cracked flashing.

Poor ventilation is another issue that can affect roofing systems. Without proper airflow, heat and moisture become trapped in the attic, leading to mold growth and higher energy bills. Clogged gutters and storm debris can also contribute to roof deterioration if left untreated.

Professional roofing contractors in Summerville perform detailed inspections to detect these problems early. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of the roof and reduces the risk of costly repairs in the future.
